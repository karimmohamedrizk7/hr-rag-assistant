Promotion Policy
Promotions are considered once per year, with decisions announced in January alongside the salary review. To be eligible, an employee must have spent at least 18 months in their current role, received a performance rating of 4 or above in the last two reviews, and have no active disciplinary warnings. The direct manager nominates the employee, and a promotion committee made of HR and department heads reviews the nominations. A promotion usually comes with a salary increase of 10% to 20%, depending on the level change. Employees may also apply for internal job openings, which are posted on the portal for at least 10 days before being opened to external candidates. Employees applying internally must inform their manager.
