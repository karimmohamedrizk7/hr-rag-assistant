Attendance and Lateness Policy
Employees must record attendance using the fingerprint system at the start and end of each day. A 15-minute grace period is allowed, up to 3 times per month. After the third time, each late arrival is deducted from the employee's salary proportionally to the minutes missed. Arriving more than 60 minutes late counts as half a day of absence unless approved in advance. Three days of unexcused absence in a row, or five days in a year, are considered a serious violation and may lead to a formal warning. If an employee will be absent, they must inform their direct manager before 9:00 AM on the same day. Forgetting to record attendance must be reported to HR within 48 hours.
