Sick Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to paid sick leave when they present a medical report from a company-approved or government-recognized clinic. Sick leave is paid at 75% of salary for the first 90 days in a year and at 85% for the following 90 days, as long as the medical report supports it. The employee must notify their manager on the first day of illness and submit the medical report to HR within 3 working days. Sick leave of more than 3 consecutive days requires a detailed medical report. Chronic or long-term conditions are reviewed by the company doctor, and reasonable adjustments to the workplace may be arranged. Abuse of sick leave, such as fake reports, is treated as a serious violation.
