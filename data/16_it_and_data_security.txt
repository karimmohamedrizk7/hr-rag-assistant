IT and Data Security Policy
Company devices and accounts are for work use, with limited personal use allowed if it does not affect work. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email and the VPN. Employees must lock their screen when leaving their desk, and must not install unapproved software. Customer and employee personal data are classified as confidential and must not be stored on personal devices or sent to personal email accounts. Suspicious emails must be reported to the IT help desk at extension 100 immediately. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 2 hours. The use of public AI tools with confidential company data is not allowed. Violations may lead to disciplinary action.
