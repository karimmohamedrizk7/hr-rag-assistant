Overtime Policy
Overtime must be approved in advance by the direct manager and recorded in the system. Overtime on regular working days is paid at 135% of the normal hourly rate, and night overtime (between 7:00 PM and 7:00 AM) is paid at 170%. Work on Fridays and Saturdays, or on official holidays, is paid at 200% of the normal hourly rate, or the employee may choose an equal compensatory day off. The maximum overtime allowed is 2 hours per day and 40 hours per month. Employees at manager level and above are not eligible for overtime pay. Overtime without prior approval is not paid, except in documented emergencies approved afterwards by the department head within 24 hours.
