Annual Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 21 working days of paid annual leave per year after completing 6 months of service. Employees who have completed 10 years of service, or who are over 50 years old, receive 30 working days. Leave requests must be submitted through the employee portal at least 7 days in advance, and the direct manager must respond within 3 working days. Up to 10 unused days may be carried over to the next year, and any additional days expire on March 31. Employees may not take more than 10 consecutive working days without approval from the department head. Unused leave that has not expired is paid out when employment ends. Public holidays are not counted as annual leave.
