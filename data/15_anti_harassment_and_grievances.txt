Anti-Harassment and Grievance Procedure
Nile Industries has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or bullying based on gender, religion, age, disability or any other personal characteristic. Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it to HR, the Ethics Committee, or the confidential hotline at ethics@nileindustries.example. All complaints are investigated within 15 working days by a team that is independent of the employee's department. The identity of the person reporting is kept confidential, and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself a serious violation. For normal workplace grievances, employees should first speak with their direct manager, then escalate to HR if not resolved within 10 working days, and then to the HR Director.
