Bonuses and Incentives Policy
Eligible employees may receive an annual performance bonus of between 0 and 3 months of basic salary, depending on the company results and their individual performance rating. Employees with a rating of "Needs Improvement" are not eligible. The bonus is paid in March after the financial results are approved. New joiners who completed at least 6 months in the previous year receive a proportional bonus. The company also offers a referral bonus of 5,000 EGP for each successful hire referred by an employee, paid after the new hire completes 3 months. An annual "Employee of the Year" award includes a cash prize of 15,000 EGP. Bonuses are discretionary and may change if the company's financial situation changes.
