Salary and Payroll Policy
Salaries are paid on the 25th of every month by bank transfer. If the 25th falls on a Friday or a public holiday, payment is made on the last working day before it. Each employee receives a digital payslip showing the basic salary, allowances, overtime, deductions, taxes and net pay. Standard allowances include a transport allowance of 800 EGP and a meal allowance of 600 EGP per month. Deductions include income tax, social insurance contributions, and unpaid absence. Salary reviews take place once a year in January. Questions or errors in the payslip must be reported to payroll@nileindustries.example within 30 days of receiving it, and corrections are made in the next payroll cycle.
