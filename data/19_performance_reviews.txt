Performance Review Policy
Performance is reviewed twice per year: a mid-year check-in in July and a full annual review in December. At the start of the year, each employee agrees on 4 to 6 objectives with their manager. The review uses a 5-point rating scale: 5 Outstanding, 4 Exceeds Expectations, 3 Meets Expectations, 2 Needs Improvement, and 1 Unsatisfactory. The final rating is based on 70% objectives and 30% behaviors and values. Employees complete a self-assessment before the meeting and may add comments to the final review document. An employee who disagrees with a rating may appeal to HR within 10 working days. Employees rated 2 or below are placed on a 90-day Performance Improvement Plan with clear targets and monthly follow-up.
