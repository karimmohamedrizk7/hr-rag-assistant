Maternity, Paternity and Parental Leave
Female employees are entitled to 120 days of fully paid maternity leave, which can begin up to 45 days before the expected delivery date. This benefit is available to employees who have completed at least 10 months of service. After returning, nursing mothers receive two paid nursing breaks of 30 minutes each per day for up to 24 months. Male employees receive 5 days of fully paid paternity leave, to be taken within the first month after birth. Employees may also request up to 6 months of unpaid childcare leave, up to 3 times during their career. Requests for maternity leave should be sent to HR with a medical certificate at least 30 days before the leave start date.
