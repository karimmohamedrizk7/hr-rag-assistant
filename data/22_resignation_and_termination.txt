Resignation and Termination Policy
An employee who wishes to resign must submit a written resignation to the direct manager and HR. The notice period is 30 days for staff and 60 days for managers and above. The company may waive part of the notice period, or pay in lieu of it. During the notice period, the employee must hand over tasks and return all company property such as laptop, access cards and keys. HR conducts an exit interview, and final settlement is paid within 14 days after the last working day. The final settlement includes unpaid salary, unused annual leave, and any bonus due. The company may terminate employment for poor performance after a documented improvement plan, for redundancy with 60 days' notice and severance of one month's salary per year of service, or for gross misconduct following an investigation.
