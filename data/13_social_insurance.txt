Social Insurance and Pension
The company registers every employee with the national social insurance system within 30 days of starting work. Social insurance contributions are shared: the employee pays 11% and the company pays 18.75% of the insured salary. Insured salary includes basic salary and fixed allowances. In addition, the company runs a voluntary provident fund: employees may contribute 3% to 7% of basic salary and the company matches the contribution at 100% up to 5%. Matched contributions vest fully after 3 years of service. Employees can check their social insurance status from the annual statement issued by HR in February. The provident fund is paid out on retirement, resignation after vesting, or in case of death to the registered beneficiary.
