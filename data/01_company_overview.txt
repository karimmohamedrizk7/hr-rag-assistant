Nile Industries Co. - Company Overview and Employee Handbook Introduction
Nile Industries Co. is a fictional manufacturing and trading company founded in 2005 with about 850 employees across three sites: the Head Office, the Main Factory, and the Logistics Hub. This handbook explains employee rights, duties and benefits. The HR Department is located on the 3rd floor of the Head Office and can be contacted at hr@nileindustries.example or extension 300. The handbook applies to all full-time employees. Contractors and interns follow their own agreements. The company may update this handbook, and employees are notified by email at least 14 days before a change takes effect. The latest version is always on the employee portal.
