Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to act honestly, respect colleagues, and protect the reputation of the company. Employees must avoid conflicts of interest, and any outside job or business must be disclosed to HR before starting. Accepting gifts worth more than 1,000 EGP from suppliers or customers is prohibited and must be reported. Confidential company information must not be shared outside the company, during and after employment. Using company property for personal profit is prohibited. Violations are handled through a progressive disciplinary system: verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, and then termination. Serious violations such as theft, fraud, violence or working under the influence of drugs may lead to immediate termination after an investigation.
