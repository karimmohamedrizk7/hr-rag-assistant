Remote Work Policy
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing the 3-month probation period. Roles that require physical presence, such as factory operators, security and warehouse staff, are not eligible. Remote work days must be agreed with the direct manager and entered in the employee portal by the previous Thursday. Remote employees must be available online between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM and respond to messages within 30 minutes during this time. The company provides a laptop but does not pay for home internet, except for employees with a signed full-remote agreement, who receive a monthly allowance of 500 EGP. Company data must only be accessed through the company VPN on a secured network. The manager may revoke remote work privileges if performance declines.
