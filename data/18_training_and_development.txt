Training and Development Policy
Each employee has an annual training budget of 10,000 EGP and is expected to complete at least 20 hours of training per year. Training may include internal workshops, online courses, certifications, and external conferences. Employees request training through the portal with the manager's approval, and HR confirms the budget. For external programs costing more than 15,000 EGP, the employee must sign a retention agreement and remain with the company for 12 months after the program, otherwise they must repay a proportion of the cost. The company reimburses 100% of professional certification exam fees on the first successful attempt. Employees who complete a relevant master's degree with company sponsorship have a 2-year retention requirement.
