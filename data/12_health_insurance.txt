Health Insurance Policy
All full-time employees are covered by private health insurance starting from their first day of work. The insurance covers outpatient visits, hospital stays, emergency care, medications, lab tests and maternity care, with a yearly limit of 250,000 EGP per person. Employees may add their spouse and up to three children under 25 years old at the company's discount rate, and the employee pays 40% of the premium for each dependent. Dental and optical care are covered up to 8,000 EGP per year. Pre-existing conditions are covered after 12 months. Employees receive an insurance card within 10 working days, and claims should be submitted through the insurer's mobile app. Elective cosmetic procedures are not covered.
