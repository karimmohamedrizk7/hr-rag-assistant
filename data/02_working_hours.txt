Working Hours Policy
The standard working week is Sunday to Thursday. Official working hours are 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM, including a one-hour unpaid lunch break, which gives 7 net working hours per day and 35 hours per week. Factory shift employees work in three shifts: morning (6:00 AM - 2:00 PM), evening (2:00 PM - 10:00 PM), and night (10:00 PM - 6:00 AM). Shift assignments are published every Wednesday for the following week. During Ramadan, working hours are reduced by one hour per day for all employees. Employees may request flexible start times between 7:30 AM and 9:30 AM with manager approval, provided the full 7 net hours are completed.
