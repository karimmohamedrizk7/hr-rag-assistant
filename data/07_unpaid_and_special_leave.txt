Special Leave and Unpaid Leave
Employees receive paid special leave in these cases: marriage, 5 working days; death of a first-degree relative (parent, spouse, child), 5 working days; death of a second-degree relative, 3 working days; Hajj pilgrimage, 30 days once during employment after 5 years of service. Employees may take one 3-day paid leave for the birth or adoption of a family member. Unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year may be granted for personal reasons with approval from the department head and HR. During unpaid leave, health insurance continues but salary and seniority-based benefits are paused. Leave for exams is granted to employees who are studying, with a maximum of 10 days per year and proof of exam schedule.
