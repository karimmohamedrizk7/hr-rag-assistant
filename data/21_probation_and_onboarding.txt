Probation and Onboarding
New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. During probation, either side may end the employment with 7 days' written notice. Employees on probation are not eligible for annual leave or remote work until the period ends, but are covered by health insurance from day one. Onboarding in the first week includes a company orientation, IT setup, a safety briefing, and a meeting with the assigned buddy. Managers hold review meetings at 30, 60 and 90 days. At the end of the probation, the manager submits a written evaluation to HR and the employee is confirmed, extended once for up to 3 more months, or not confirmed. Employees must provide the required documents (ID copy, certificates, military status, and bank account) to HR in the first week.
