Expense and Business Travel Policy
Business trips require approval from the direct manager at least 5 working days in advance. The company books flights and hotels through the approved travel agency. Domestic trips have a daily allowance of 700 EGP, and international trips have a daily allowance of 80 USD, covering meals and local transport. Economy class is used for flights shorter than 6 hours, and business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours for directors and above. Hotels are capped at 3,500 EGP per night domestically and 150 USD per night internationally. Expense reports with original receipts must be submitted within 14 days after returning, and reimbursement is made in the next payroll. Alcohol, fines and personal entertainment are not reimbursed.
